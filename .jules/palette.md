## $(date +%Y-%m-%d) - Accessible Canvas
**Learning:** In React Three Fiber applications, the base `<Canvas>` element renders as a standard `<canvas>` without semantic meaning or focus states. This means the 3D scene is completely invisible to screen readers and unreachable by keyboard navigation.
**Action:** Always add `aria-label` and `tabIndex={0}` to `<Canvas>` components. To provide visible feedback for keyboard users without disrupting the visual design for mouse users, apply Tailwind's `focus-visible` utility classes directly to the `className` prop of the `<Canvas>` (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-4 focus-visible:ring-inset focus-visible:ring-orange-500 focus-visible:outline-none`). Use `ring-inset` to ensure the focus ring remains visible when the canvas occupies the full viewport.
